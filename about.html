About

My background and goals

Civil engineering, self-taught programming, and a few languages along the way.

Education & background

I am a Civil Engineering student at the International University of East Africa, in Kampala, Uganda. Before that, I studied Automobile Engineering starting in 2015, building a foundation in vehicle maintenance, diagnostics, and mechanical repair, and earned my high school diploma in 2023.

In 2024, I relocated to Kampala to focus on advanced English communication and professional development, believing that clear communication is one of the most powerful tools for leadership and international collaboration. Alongside my engineering studies, I taught myself web development and programming — starting with HTML and CSS, then moving into JavaScript and Python.

What I'm currently learning

I'm deepening my understanding of JavaScript logic and structures, exploring Python for data and automation tasks, and learning how civil engineering principles — load calculations, material ratios, structural limits — can be turned into simple, usable software tools. I'm also continuing to study C++ fundamentals.

My goals

My long-term goal is to become someone who can move comfortably between construction sites and codebases — an engineer who builds physical structures and the software tools that support that work. I also want to keep growing as an English communication professional and educator, since teaching English has shown me how much I enjoy helping other people build skills from the ground up, the same way I've had to build mine.

I'm trilingual — fluent in English, French, and Swahili — and I see that as an asset for working across borders, whether in engineering, software, or teaching.
